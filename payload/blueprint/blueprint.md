# Customs and Trade Regulatory Compliance

*AI-enabled transformation of International Agreements and Origin (الاتفاقيات والمنشأ) operations for الإدارة العامة لجمارك أبوظبي (Abu Dhabi Customs)*

**Target Outcome:** Faster, more consistent application of trade agreements and rules-of-origin determinations across the قسم الاتفاقيات الدولية — targeting reduced processing cycle time and improved compliance accuracy [specific baseline/target metrics not stated in source documents — ASSUMPTION pending volume and KPI data]



## Phase 1: Cognitive Deconstruction

### 1.1 Atomic Thinking Step Register

The قسم الاتفاقيات الدولية (International Agreements Section) governs ten sub-processes under دليل عمليات إدارة الاتفاقيات والمنشأ (TA-CA-10-20). The register below decomposes the core cognitive pathway spanning advance origin ruling study (TA-CA-10-20-05), ruling issuance (TA-CA-10-20-06), agreement compliance study (TA-CA-10-20-01), system updates (TA-CA-10-20-02), inquiry response (TA-CA-10-20-03/04), and dispute adjudication (TA-CA-10-20-07/08). The process is predominantly deterministic — intake validation, routing, notification dispatch, logging, and posting to منظومة العمليات المؤسسية المتكاملة (Integrated Corporate Operations System) are coded logic. AI/LLM reasoning is reserved for the genuine interpretation and synthesis steps: reading free-text advance-ruling requests, synthesizing rules-of-origin determinations against trade-agreement text, drafting compliance reports, and interpreting inbound inquiries.

| Step | Name | Type | Zone | Reliability | Design Rationale |
|------|------|------|------|-------------|------------------|
| ATS1 | Receive and register request/inquiry | DETERMINISTIC | GREEN | 99.9% | Intake of an advance-ruling request (طلب حكم مسبق للمنشأ), inquiry, or update list (قائمة التحديثات) from the digital platform; assigns a case reference and timestamp. Pure capture and record creation — no judgment. |
| ATS2 | Validate request completeness and eligibility | DETERMINISTIC | GREEN | 99.9% | Rule check that mandatory fields (applicant identity, goods description, declared origin, supporting documents) are present and the request type is in scope for the قسم. Fixed checklist; deterministic pass/fail. |
| ATS3 | Classify request type and route to sub-process | DETERMINISTIC | GREEN | 99.9% | Maps the case to one of the TA-CA-10-20 sub-process codes (origin ruling, external inquiry, internal inquiry, dispute, update) and routes to the correct queue. Lookup on request attributes; no interpretation. |
| ATS4 | Extract and structure request content | AI / LLM | AMBER | 92%+ | Reads the free-text and attached documents (invoices, bills of material, manufacturing descriptions) to extract goods characteristics, inputs, processing steps, and claimed tariff/HS context. Genuine unstructured-text interpretation beyond rule capture. |
| ATS5 | Retrieve applicable agreement and origin rules | DETERMINISTIC | GREEN | 99.9% | Pulls the relevant free-trade / bilateral / GCC / WCO / WTO agreement clauses (بنود الاتفاقية) and rules-of-origin tables for the declared country pair and goods category from the knowledge base. | ATS10 | Route decision for approval | DETERMINISTIC | GREEN | 99.9% | Pushes the drafted ruling/report into the approval workflow (section head → committee → sector executive director as applicable) based on decision type and value thresholds. Deterministic workflow routing. |
| ATS11 | Capture human approval / modification decision | DETERMINISTIC | AMBER | 99.9% | Records the reviewer's approve / reject / modify action on the drafted decision. The human judgment occurs outside the step; the step deterministically captures and enforces the gate. |

Deterministic write and dissemination. |

| ATS14 | Adjudicate agreement/origin dispute escalation | AI / LLM | RED | 90%+ | For disputes escalated from a customs centre (خلاف في المراكز الجمركية) or objections referred to the اللجنة الفنية (Technical Committee), synthesizes the competing positions and prior ruling to prepare a recommended adjudication. High legal/financial exposure and binding effect mandate immediate human escalation — never autonomous. |

### 1.3 Autonomy Zone Map

| Zone | Count | Percentage | Steps |
|------|-------|------------|-------|
| **GREEN** | 9 | 64% | ATS1, ATS2, ATS3, ATS5, ATS7, ATS9, ATS10, ATS12, ATS13 |
| **AMBER** | 4 | 29% | ATS4, ATS6, ATS8, ATS11 |
| **RED** | 1 | 7% | ATS14 |


Of the 14 atomic steps, 9 are classified GREEN (ATS1, ATS2, ATS3, ATS5, ATS7, ATS9, ATS10, ATS12, ATS13), representing 64% of the register. These are the deterministic backbone — intake, validation, routing, agreement/rule retrieval, classification reconciliation, legal-constraint stamping, approval routing, system-of-record posting, dissemination, and notification dispatch — executing autonomously at 99.9% reliability with full audit logging in منظومة العمليات المؤسسية المتكاملة.

Four steps are classified AMBER (ATS4, ATS6, ATS8, ATS11), representing 29%. These cover the AI-powered interpretation of request content, the rule-of-origin determination, the drafting of rulings and compliance reports, and the human approval gate. In each case the agent recommends and a qualified reviewer in the قسم الاتفاقيات الدولية approves before the decision becomes binding — consistent with the operational-risk controls RSK-OPS-PPL-178 (delay/accuracy of advance-assessment decisions) and RSK-OPS-PPL-05 (data-entry error controls) documented in the process manual.

One step is classified RED (ATS14), representing 7% — the adjudication of escalated agreement/origin disputes and objections referred to the اللجنة الفنية. Given the binding legal effect, duty/financial exposure, and the high volume of objections flagged under risk RSK-OPS-PRO-313 (كثرة الاعتراض), this step is a hard stop requiring mandatory human intervention; no autonomous action is permitted.

**Promotion rule:** All AI steps begin in AMBER (RED for ATS14). An AMBER step is promotable to GREEN only once its eval baselines mature above 97% sustained reliability for that specific step over a defined production sample, with zero autonomy violations. ATS14 is not eligible for promotion from RED while its binding legal and financial exposure persists.

## Phase 2: Context & Memory Engineering

### 2.1 Context Payload Specifications

The following specifications define exactly what each AI-powered agent receives at inference time. Context payloads are engineered to the precision principle: each AI step (ATS4, ATS6, ATS8, ATS14) receives only the scoped, freshness-bounded data it needs to reason, drawn from the منظومة العمليات المؤسسية المتكاملة (Integrated Corporate Operations System) as system of record and the agreements/origin knowledge base governed by دليل عمليات إدارة الاتفاقيات والمنشأ (TA-CA-10-20). All payloads are bounded by a maximum token budget and ordered so that the governing legal framework and request-specific facts appear first for optimal model attention.

| AI-Powered Step | Context Payload | Detailed Description |
|-----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| ATS4 — Extract and structure request content | Raw request artifact + request-type schema + extraction exemplars | The agent receives: the full raw inbound request/inquiry document (advance-origin-ruling request, external inquiry, internal inquiry, or dispute filing) as submitted to قسم الاتفاقيات الدولية, including any attachments (commercial invoice, bill of materials, product specifications, prior correspondence); the detected language of the submission (Arabic/English); the canonical request-type schema defining required fields (applicant identity, HS/tariff hint, declared country of origin, goods description, intended import date, supporting evidence list); 3–5 structured-extraction exemplars for each request type; and the registration metadata produced by ATS1 (request ID, receipt timestamp, channel). The agent does NOT receive the agreements rule corpus or any origin-determination logic. Freshness: current transaction only. Budget: ≤ 12,000 tokens. |
| ATS6 — Determine rule-of-origin satisfaction | Structured request facts + applicable agreement rule set (from ATS5) + product BOM + prior rulings on same product family | The agent receives: the structured goods description, bill of materials and value breakdown extracted by ATS4; the specific applicable agreement(s) and their rules-of-origin text retrieved deterministically by ATS5 (GCC Common Customs Law provisions, bilateral/multilateral FTA origin rules, اتفاقية تسهيل التجارة and اتفاقية كيوتو المعدلة provisions as scoped); the applicable origin criteria (wholly-obtained, substantial transformation, change-in-tariff-heading, regional value content thresholds) with their numeric thresholds; the verified HS/tariff classification consistency result from ATS7 where available; and episodic history of prior advance rulings (قرار حكم مسبق للمنشأ) issued for the same product family or applicant. The agent does NOT receive unrelated agreement texts outside the scoped set. Freshness: agreement rules real-time from knowledge base; prior rulings T+1. Budget: ≤ 20,000 tokens. |
| ATS8 — Draft advance ruling / compliance report | Origin determination result (from ATS6) + request facts + decision template + legal-language library + validity/binding constraints | The agent receives: the rule-of-origin satisfaction conclusion and its reasoning chain from ATS6; the structured request facts and applicant identity; the governing decision document template (قرار حكم مسبق للمنشأ or امتثال تقرير) with mandatory sections; the approved legal-language library and standard clauses for binding scope, validity period, and appeal rights under the المسبقة الأحكام framework; references to the specific agreement articles cited in the determination; and the منظومة العمليات المؤسسية المتكاملة reference number. The agent produces a complete draft decision; it does NOT finalize or apply binding legal constraints (that is deterministic ATS9). Freshness: template and clause library real-time; request facts current transaction. Budget: ≤ 16,000 tokens. |
| ATS14 — Adjudicate agreement/origin dispute escalation | Full dispute dossier + both-party submissions + prior determination + all applicable agreement provisions + دليل التدقيق precedent set | The agent receives: the complete dispute dossier escalated from a customs center (المركز الجمركي) or from الإدارة, including the متعامل's claim and supporting evidence, the customs center's position, the original origin determination and its reasoning, all applicable agreement and GCC Common Customs Law provisions in scope, the complete chain of prior correspondence, any related prior rulings for the same goods, and the precedent set of comparable adjudicated disputes with their outcomes and rationale. The agent produces a reasoned recommended adjudication; because this is a RED-zone step, the recommendation is presented to the اللجنة الفنية (Technical Committee) for binding decision and the agent takes no autonomous action. Freshness: dispute dossier current; agreement provisions and precedent set real-time. Budget: ≤ 28,000 tokens. |

### 2.2 Memory Architecture Configuration

Each AI agent is configured across the three memory tiers. Session memory holds in-flight transaction state and is discarded at task completion. Episodic memory persists per-entity history (per-applicant/متعامل, per-product-family, per-agreement, per-dispute) to improve consistency across requests. Semantic memory captures cross-entity learned patterns and is refreshed on a governed cadence. All tiers are tenant-isolated within the منظومة العمليات المؤسسية المتكاملة environment and tagged for regulatory sensitivity. The deterministic agents (AGN1, AGN5) are included for completeness; their memory needs are bounded to session state and reference-data caching since they perform no probabilistic reasoning.

### 2.3 Audit Trace Architecture Specification

Retention horizons align with the binding-ruling precedent horizon and dispute-appeal windows. ### 3.5 Orchestration Topology

The workflow runs on a deterministic backbone with surgical AI insertion, coordinated by a supervisor that holds case state in منظومة العمليات المؤسسية المتكاملة as the system of record. Execution begins with AGN1 (Intake & Routing Agent) running ATS1→ATS2→ATS3 strictly sequentially through the Deterministic Rule Engine: a case is registered, validated for completeness and eligibility, then classified and routed to the correct sub-process branch. These GREEN deterministic steps never touch an LLM — they execute as rule/lookup/routing logic and either advance the case or reject/return it to the applicant with a structured completeness notice.

Where the routing classifies the request as one requiring content interpretation (advance ruling requests, substantive inquiries, disputes), the supervisor invokes AGN2 (Content Extraction Agent) for ATS4. This is the first AMBER checkpoint: the agent's structured extraction carries per-field confidence, and any field below the confidence threshold holds for human review before the case proceeds. Once the structured request object is confirmed, control passes to AGN3 (Rules & Origin Agent). Within AGN3, ATS5 (rule retrieval) and ATS7 (classification-consistency verification) run deterministically, while ATS6 (rule-of-origin satisfaction) is the core AMBER reasoning step — the agent produces a cited determination that is held at an Amber checkpoint for reviewer validation of the origin rationale before any ruling is drafted.

AGN4 (Ruling Drafting Agent) then executes ATS8 (draft generation, AMBER) followed by ATS9 (validity/legal-constraint application, deterministic GREEN) in sequence; the deterministic constraint application always runs after and over the AI-generated draft so legal validity windows and binding scope cannot be hallucinated. The drafted, constraint-annotated decision flows to AGN5 (Decision & Issuance Agent), which runs the deterministic sequence ATS10 (route for approval) → ATS11 (capture human approval/modification — AMBER human gate) → ATS12 (issue and post to system of record) → ATS13 (dispatch notifications). ATS11 is a mandatory human decision gate: the platform holds and no issuance (ATS12) or notification (ATS13) occurs until the authorized approver records a decision. Notification dispatch and system-of-record posting can run in parallel fan-out to applicant and relevant customs centres once approval is captured.

The dispute branch is handled separately: when ATS3 routes a case as a dispute escalation, AGN6 (Dispute Adjudication Agent) executes ATS14 in the RED zone. This is a hard stop — AGN6 synthesizes a reasoned, cited recommendation but takes no autonomous action; the case is packaged and escalated immediately to the اللجنة الفنية (Technical Committee) for mandatory human/committee decision, after which the outcome re-enters the deterministic issuance path via AGN5. Across all branches the supervisor enforces state transitions, A2a handoff contracts (structured request object → determination → draft → decision), and escalation routing, ensuring every AMBER/RED junction is verified before downstream steps execute.

## Phase 5: Agentic Activation

### 5.1 Change Management & User Enablement

The activation of agentic capabilities within the قسم الاتفاقيات الدولية والملكية الفكرية (International Agreements and Intellectual Property Section) under إدارة السياسات والحوكمة represents a shift from fully manual origin determination and agreement administration to an AI-augmented operating model where staff transition from performing routine extraction, retrieval, and drafting tasks to validating and approving agent-generated recommendations. Change management is grounded in the operational risk register of دليل عمليات إدارة الاتفاقيات والمنشأ (TA-CA-10-20), which already identifies staff turnover (RSK-OPS-PPL-07), lack of professional and technical training (RSK-OPS-PPL-04), and delay in responding to inquiries (RSK-OPS-PPL-177) as material risks — the enablement program directly mitigates each of these by embedding knowledge into agents and reskilling staff into higher-value review roles.

Role change communication will be delivered through structured briefings led by رئيس قسم الاتفاقيات الدولية والملكية الفكرية (Head of the International Agreements and Intellectual Property Section), explaining what is changing (which atomic steps become agent-assisted), why (faster, more consistent application of trade agreements and rules-of-origin determinations), and what is expected of each role under the new model. The AMBER review workbenches for the extraction agent (AGN2, ATS4), origin determination agent (AGN3, ATS6), and ruling drafting agent (AGN4, ATS8), together with the AMBER approval capture step (ATS11) owned by the decision issuance agent (AGN5) and the RED dispute adjudication escalation (AGN6, ATS14), require reviewers to be trained on the human-in-the-loop interfaces, the confidence indicators each agent surfaces, the evidence trace behind each recommendation, and the escalation procedures to the اللجنة الفنية (Technical Committee) defined in process TA-CA-10-20-10.

Reviewer training is role-specific and competency-based: origin specialists are trained to interpret the rule-of-origin satisfaction reasoning (ATS6) against the applicable agreement text and rules-of-origin retrieved at ATS5; legal and policy staff are trained to validate the validity, binding scope, and legal constraints applied at ATS9 against POL-PG-IPR-01 and the WCO/Trade Facilitation advance-ruling guidance before an advance ruling is approved at ATS11. End-user onboarding for internal and external inquiry workflows (processes TA-CA-10-20-03 and TA-CA-10-20-04) covers how requests are received and registered (ATS1), how automated routing (ATS3) directs work, and how to interpret agent-drafted compliance responses. Feedback channels — a structured override-reason capture form embedded in each AMBER workbench, fortnightly review clinics, and an adoption survey — feed override patterns back into the improvement backlog and support Amber→Green promotion decisions. Adoption metrics tracked from day one include reviewer workbench utilization rate, average review time per decision, override rate per agent, and reviewer confidence scores collected through the survey.
